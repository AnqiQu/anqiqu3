---
title: Notes on Attention
date: 2026-06-18
---

Attention is the rarest and purest form of generosity. We say we don't have
time, but what we really don't have is attention — the willingness to let one
thing be, for a while, the only thing.

Everything I care about downstream of that: the work, the friendships, the
ideas worth keeping. They all begin with the decision to look, and to keep
looking after the first novelty wears off.

This is a short one. The point is the point.
