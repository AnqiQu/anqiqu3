date: 2026-07-22

A short manifesto, mostly to hold myself to it.

## Principles

1. **Make the thing before talking about the thing.** A rough prototype beats a
   polished description almost every time.
2. **Optimize for the second draft.** The first draft only has to exist. Its job
   is to be improvable, not good.
3. **Keep the surface small.** Every feature, every abstraction, every promise is
   a thing that must be maintained. Say no by default.
4. **Leave the campsite better.** Touch a file, improve a file — even a comment,
   even a name.

## What I refuse

- Cleverness that costs the next reader more than it saved me.
- Meetings that could have been a decision.
- Perfection as a form of procrastination.

None of this is original. All of it is hard. That's why it's a manifesto and not
a to-do list.
