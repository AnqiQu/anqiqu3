# Anqi Intelligence favicon concepts

These are exploratory assets only. They do not replace or modify the favicons in `public/`.

All source icons use a `64 64` viewBox, flat colors, crisp geometry, and an optical safe area of roughly 8 units. The exported PNGs are deliberately rendered at 32×32 so each idea can be judged close to browser-tab size.

## Shared system

- **Server Room:** black (`#000000`), white (`#FFFFFF`), and signal green (`#80ED99`).
- **Sandbox:** sky blue (`#1A96F2`), leaf green (`#52A842` / `#86D462`), sun cream (`#FFFDF0`), and earth (`#7D5F43`).
- The pairs share their outer silhouette and proportions. Dark, typographic interiors identify Server Room; bright, landscape-based interiors identify Sandbox.

## Concept 01 — Signal tile / Island signal

- **Server Room — Signal AQ:** The most direct monogram. A broad white A and green Q sit as two readable signal blocks on a black rounded tile. The Q tail points up and right, adding energy without circuitry.
- **Sandbox — Island signal:** The same rounded tile becomes sky. A circular sun and a centered floating-island diamond reuse the Server Room icon's circle/diagonal vocabulary.

## Concept 02 — Diamond core / Diamond world

- **Server Room — Diamond AQ:** A transparent-background black diamond contains a compact white A and a green square Q. Its silhouette is unusually easy to pick out among rounded browser-tab icons.
- **Sandbox — Diamond world:** The same diamond becomes a tiny world window: blue sky, cream sun, green land, and brown floating rock.

## Concept 03 — Console gate / Garden gate

- **Server Room — Console gate:** Green side rails suggest a precise terminal or server bay. A white A and a compact green Q share the protected central bay without fine linework.
- **Sandbox — Garden gate:** The rails turn sky blue and frame a two-leaf sprout growing from a single earth block. The white tile keeps this pair bright and especially crisp.

## Concept 04 — Hex node / Hex habitat

- **Server Room — Hex node:** A black hexagonal badge carries the simplest AQ construction in the set: white A, green Q. The transparent corners make the mark feel like a technical module rather than an app tile.
- **Sandbox — Hex habitat:** The matching hexagon becomes a sky habitat containing a sun, rounded canopy, and a floating earth point.

## Recommendation

**Concept 05 — Inverse A** is the strongest coordinated pair. The identical A creates immediate brand unity, the dark-versus-light inversion distinguishes contexts at a glance, and both icons retain exceptional clarity under aggressive downscaling.

Concept 02 is the strongest alternate when representing the two environments pictorially is more important than absolute reduction.

## Concept 05 — Inverse A

- **Server Room — Black A tile:** A single broad white A on black. This is the most reduced expression of the technical identity and has maximum contrast at favicon scale.
- **Sandbox — Green A tile:** The exact same A silhouette changes to meadow green on a warm daylight background. The shared letterform makes the relationship immediate; the color inversion separates the two spaces without adding detail.

This is the strongest option when brand unity and small-size clarity matter more than representing each environment pictorially. It is also the easiest pair to maintain across favicon, app-icon, and social-avatar sizes.
